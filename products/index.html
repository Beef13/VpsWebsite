Quality Pallets in Melbourne & Victoria

Browse our range of Australian Standard, Export ISPM15, Euro, Block, and Custom pallets. Available in new and recycled options, with heavy, medium, and light weight capacities to suit your needs.

Australian Standard Pallets (1165x1165mm)

Our most popular pallet size, the Australian Standard pallet measures 1165mm x 1165mm and is ideal for general warehouse use, retail distribution, and local transport. Available in heavy weight (1500kg capacity), medium weight (1000kg capacity), and light weight (500kg capacity).

Export Pallets ISPM15 (1140x1140mm)

All our export pallets are ISPM15 certified and heat-treated for international shipping compliance. Manufactured in-house to your specifications with 1000kg capacity. Perfect for container shipping worldwide.

Second Hand & Recycled Pallets

Quality recycled timber pallets, repaired with new timber boards. Environmentally friendly and cost-effective option available in all weight capacities. Great for warehouse storage and one-way shipping.

Custom Pallets & Non-Standard Sizes

We manufacture custom-sized pallets including 1100x1100mm, 1150x1150mm, Euro pallets (1200x800mm), and block pallets. Contact us to discuss your specific requirements.
